How Clean Co Works

Three simple steps to a cleaner community.

🗑️

1. General waste collection

Regular collection of household, commercial and industrial waste.

♲

2. Rycycling

We sort, process and transform waste to reusable materials.

🏥

3. Hospital waste disposal

Collection and destruction of hospital waste to prevent the spread of infections.

🌱

4. Organic composting

Food and garden wastes composting and biogas production.

💻

5. E-Waste disposal

Safe handling, recycling, throwing away used electronics. We also recycle materials such as copper.

🧴

6. Sanitation Services

Street sweeping, drain and sewer cleaning.
