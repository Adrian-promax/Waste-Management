About Clean Co Ngong

Sasa! Ever walked past that overflowing bin at Ngong Market and just thought, “Aii, surely by now someone should have cleared this?” Adrian did—and that’s how Clean Co Ngong was born. It started with one guy and a big idea: what if reporting trash in our mtaa was as easy as taking a selfie?

Why we do this

We love Ngong, simple as that. We want our home to be as fresh as the sunrise over Ngong Hills. But sometimes, after a busy market day or a downpour, rubbish piles up—plastic bottles, blue paper bags, maize husks—making the place look a bit less like home.
Adrian and a few friends thought, “Why not make it super easy for everyone to call out these messes?” No long forms. No accounts. No stress. Just see, snap, and share—then we all pitch in!

Who’s behind Clean Co Ngong?

Meet Adrian—the founder, the guy who won’t stop talking about a cleaner Ngong. One day, after stepping over the same pile of trash by Saitoti road for the third time that week, he finally said, “Basi, enough! Let’s do something about this.”

Adrian, plus friends like Wanjiku (the one always laughing at matatu stories), Kevin (never short on jokes), and Mama Faith (her chai is still legendary), decided to get everyone involved. Now, you’ll find them at Saturday clean-ups, debating who makes the softest chapati, or planning the next big #UsafiNiWetu moment.

We believe every small effort counts. Kama kawaida, tuko pamoja!
