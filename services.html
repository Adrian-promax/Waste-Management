Sasa, Hii Ndio Tunafanya (Seriously, This Is It)

People always ask, "So you guys just collect trash?" Eish, if only it was that simple. Here’s what really goes on around here, no filters.

  • Bin Pickup
    You know that bin by Ngong Market? Ebu, if it’s full just tell us. We don’t always get there on time (traffic, si unajua), but we try. Last time, Kevin dropped his glove in the trash. He still complains. 😂
  • Recycling (Sorta)
    If it looks like it can be used again, we try – bottles, plastic, old buckets. Last time, someone brought a broken jiko. We laughed, but now it’s a flower pot. Anything goes.
  • Clinic Stuff
    Hospitals and chemists, please, just call us. Don’t throw those things in the river, bana! Mama Njeri always gives us mandazi after we collect. Sometimes she gives two if we complain enough.
  • Compost
    Banana peels, ugali, cabbage leaves… we dump them behind the church and after a while, magic. Brian swears that's why his sukuma is big. Could be.
  • Old Electronics
    That kabambe from 2003? We’ll take it. Don’t ask what we do with them, but at least it’s not in the river.
  • Street Cleaning
    We sweep, unblock drains (si it’s hard!), and sometimes just laugh at how much mud comes out. If you see us struggling, shout “pole!” or bring chai.

If you want something else cleaned, or you just want to gossip, shout here.
Sometimes we’re late, sometimes we get it wrong, but we always try. Usafi ni yetu sote, si ya serikali tu!

“Ati last time we cleaned, that goat from the stage just sat and watched. Even goats want a clean town.”
— Adrian, founder (sometimes seen with a broom, sometimes with chapati)
