Neighbors helping neighbors in Ngong

Karibu Ngong! Let’s keep our town clean
and let Ngong Hills shine.

Ever walk past a pile of rubbish near Ngong Market or along the road to Kiserian and think, “Someone should take care of this?” Now you actually can! Snap a photo, tell us where you saw it—even if it’s up on Kona Baridi or outside your apartment in Ngong town—and we’ll help get it sorted.

Just last weekend, a few of us met up after church and picked up plastic bottles around Ngong Matatu stage. Big up to Wanjiku for bringing extra gloves, and to Brian for keeping us laughing—cleaning up was way more fun together!

As Uncle Otieno always says, “Tuwe pamoja, tusafishe Ngong.” So don’t wait—report any mess, big or small. Every little bit helps make Ngong a better place for everyone!
