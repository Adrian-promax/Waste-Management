## About This Project

Ngong is full of life, stories, and (let’s be honest) sometimes a little too much rubbish.  
This website is here so **everyone** can report messes, see what’s happening around, and feel good about making the town cleaner—whether you’re reporting a mountain of plastics or just laughing about a goat watching the cleaners.

---

## Description

- **See a mess? Shout about it.**  
  Overflowing bins, sneaky plastics, boda guys tossing cans, a random fridge in the river—just fill the form and let us know. Rant, use emojis, write in Sheng, even go anonymous if you want. Hakuna pressure!

- **Everyone’s voice matters.**  
  All reports show up instantly on the site. Search by your favorite “base” or filter by category. If something gets fixed, anyone can clear the story.

- **Live stats, local flavor.**  
  The home page keeps count of every mess, urgent story, and recycling win—because small steps matter.

- **No passwords, no cloud, hakuna drama.**  
  Your reports live in your own browser (`localStorage`). No one else sees them unless you’re sharing your phone.

- **Services, but with stories.**  
  Our “services” are real: sometimes we sweep up, sometimes we get mandazi, sometimes the goat just sits and stares. You’ll see it all described in local style.

---

## Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – Every page, from the home to the forms.
- **CSS3** – Clean, simple design, easy on your eyes (and phone).
- **Vanilla JavaScript** – Handles forms, keeps reports in your browser, makes stats work, and even updates the year so we look sharp.
- **No frameworks. No backend. No libraries.**  
  If you can open a browser, you’re good to go.
